===============================================================================
 ATHAR PLATFORM - cPanel / hPanel CRON ENTRIES
 Reference copy. Explanation and verification: deploy/cron.md
===============================================================================

 BEFORE YOU PASTE ANYTHING, REPLACE THESE TWO PLACEHOLDERS IN EVERY LINE:

   /usr/bin/php        ->  the ABSOLUTE path of the PHP 8.2+ CLI binary
                           find it with:  readlink -f "$(command -v php)"
                           on Hostinger it is usually one of:
                               /opt/alt/php82/usr/bin/php
                               /opt/alt/php83/usr/bin/php
                               /usr/local/bin/php
                               /usr/bin/php
                           verify with:   <path> -v      (must print 8.2 or 8.3)
                                          <path> -m      (must list pdo_mysql, mbstring, openssl)

   /home/u123456789    ->  the real account home
                           find it with:  echo $HOME

 THEN PROVE THE COMBINATION WORKS BEFORE CREATING ANY CRON ENTRY:

   /usr/bin/php /home/u123456789/athar-app/artisan about
   /usr/bin/php /home/u123456789/athar-app/artisan schedule:list

 If either command errors, fix that first. A cron entry built on a broken
 command fails silently once a minute, forever.

===============================================================================
 SCHEDULE FIELDS (hPanel asks for these separately)
===============================================================================

   minute  hour  day-of-month  month  day-of-week
      *     *         *          *         *          = every minute

===============================================================================
 ENTRY 1 OF 4 - LARAVEL SCHEDULER          schedule: * * * * *   (every minute)
===============================================================================
 The single entry point for every scheduled task in the application, including
 the BR-08 absent sweep and the BR-09 incomplete-attendance sweep.
 Article 10: one cron entry, one minute, all tasks pass through it.

* * * * * /usr/bin/php /home/u123456789/athar-app/artisan schedule:run >> /home/u123456789/athar-app/storage/logs/cron-schedule.log 2>&1

===============================================================================
 ENTRY 2 OF 4 - QUEUE WORKER               schedule: * * * * *   (every minute)
===============================================================================
 Drains the database queue and EXITS. Never a daemon (Article 10).
 --stop-when-empty  exit as soon as the queue is empty
 --max-time=55      exit after 55s regardless, before the next minute starts
 --tries=3          three attempts, then the job lands in failed_jobs
 --backoff=30       wait 30s before retrying a failed job
 --timeout=50       per-job ceiling, must stay below --max-time
 --memory=180       restart threshold in MB, below the 256M php limit
 DO NOT REMOVE --stop-when-empty OR --max-time.

* * * * * /usr/bin/php /home/u123456789/athar-app/artisan queue:work --stop-when-empty --max-time=55 --tries=3 --backoff=30 --timeout=50 --sleep=1 --memory=180 --quiet >> /home/u123456789/athar-app/storage/logs/cron-queue.log 2>&1

 --- optional variant, only if the account's process allowance is being hit ---
 --- requires flock; check with:  command -v flock                          ---

# * * * * * /usr/bin/flock -n /home/u123456789/athar-app/storage/framework/queue.lock /usr/bin/php /home/u123456789/athar-app/artisan queue:work --stop-when-empty --max-time=55 --tries=3 --backoff=30 --timeout=50 --sleep=1 --memory=180 --quiet >> /home/u123456789/athar-app/storage/logs/cron-queue.log 2>&1

===============================================================================
 ENTRY 3 OF 4 - DAILY DATABASE BACKUP      schedule: 15 0 * * *  (00:15 UTC)
===============================================================================
 00:15 UTC = 03:15 Asia/Riyadh. Riyadh is a constant UTC+3 with no daylight
 saving, so this never needs a seasonal adjustment.
 CONFIRM THE CRON TIMEZONE FIRST:   date ; date -u
 If cron runs in Riyadh time rather than UTC, use  15 3 * * *  instead.
 Retention (30 days) is handled inside the script. See deploy/backup.md.

15 0 * * * /bin/sh /home/u123456789/athar-app/deploy/backup-db.sh >> /home/u123456789/backups/backup-db.log 2>&1

===============================================================================
 ENTRY 4 OF 4 - WEEKLY FILE BACKUP         schedule: 45 0 * * 5  (Fri 00:45 UTC)
===============================================================================
 Friday 00:45 UTC = Friday 03:45 Asia/Riyadh - the weekend in Saudi Arabia,
 the lowest-traffic window. Retention (8 weeks) is handled inside the script.

45 0 * * 5 /bin/sh /home/u123456789/athar-app/deploy/backup-files.sh >> /home/u123456789/backups/backup-files.log 2>&1

===============================================================================
 OPTIONAL - CRON LOG HOUSEKEEPING          schedule: 30 1 1 * *  (monthly)
===============================================================================
 cron-schedule.log and cron-queue.log are append-only shell logs and are NOT
 rotated by Laravel's daily log channel. Without this they grow without bound
 and eventually consume the account's inode/disk allowance.
 This truncates them to their last 2000 lines once a month.

30 1 1 * * /usr/bin/find /home/u123456789/athar-app/storage/logs -name 'cron-*.log' -size +5M -exec sh -c 'tail -n 2000 "$1" > "$1.tmp" && mv "$1.tmp" "$1"' _ {} \; >/dev/null 2>&1

===============================================================================
 WHAT MUST NEVER BE ADDED
===============================================================================
 - queue:work without --stop-when-empty and --max-time   (that is a daemon)
 - queue:listen                                          (slower, heavier)
 - schedule:work                                         (a foreground daemon)
 - a second schedule:run entry                           (one entry, one minute)
 - any entry without  >> <logfile> 2>&1                  (floods the mailbox)
 - any entry using a relative path                       (cron has no PATH)

===============================================================================
 VERIFICATION, TWO MINUTES AFTER SAVING THE ENTRIES
===============================================================================
 ls -l --time-style=full-iso /home/u123456789/athar-app/storage/logs/cron-*.log
     -> both files exist and their mtime is within the last minute or two.
        EMPTY FILES ARE NORMAL AND EXPECTED on an idle system; the mtime, not
        the contents, is the proof that cron ran.

 /usr/bin/php /home/u123456789/athar-app/artisan schedule:list
     -> wait 90 seconds, run it again; the next-run times must have advanced.

 /usr/bin/php /home/u123456789/athar-app/artisan queue:failed
     -> must be empty.

 Full troubleshooting table: deploy/cron.md section 7.
